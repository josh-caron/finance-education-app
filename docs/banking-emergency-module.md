# Banking and Emergency Savings

Follows **Money Basics: Budgeting and Saving**. Four lessons and 20 exercises
cover account choice, a starter emergency fund, account fees, and deposit
insurance. Numeric answers are derived with `@fin/core` helpers. Insurance and
fee figures are given inside each problem because those amounts are set by law
or by the institution and can change.

## Editorial references

Full table: [research-sources.md](research-sources.md).

Each unit after the first is locked in the UI until every lesson in the previous
unit is completed: budgeting unlocks banking, banking unlocks money basics.
